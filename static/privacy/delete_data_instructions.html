How to Delete Your Data

We value your privacy and provide options to delete your personal data. Follow the steps below to request data deletion:

Steps to Delete Your Data:

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Go to the Settings or Privacy section.
  3. Find the Delete Account or Remove Data option.
  4. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm the deletion.

Alternative Method:

If you cannot find the delete option, you can request data deletion by contacting us:

  • Email: support@example.com
  • Support Form: https://example.com/support

Note: Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
